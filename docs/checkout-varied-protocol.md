Three missions, three arms, three repeats: **27 cohorts / 108 actors**. Arms are current
product commit/lease rules, block modification grants, and graph-backed grants. Four
native actors prepare concurrently in separate copies in each cohort, including when
initial checkout is refused. Publication requires the selected arm's admitted write
path. Cohorts run sequentially in rotating arm order. Acquisition order rotates across
repeats and is identical in every matched arm. Each actor gets at most three native
turns; each cohort has a ten-minute deadline. A separate retained two-actor runtime
preflight verifies directory-local edits and confirmed server stop before admission.

1. **Tax/partial stock:** mixed taxable/exempt pricing, compatible v1/v2 persistence,
   repeated-SKU partial reservations, full captured payment and integrated text. The
   original 16-module synthetic seed and 14 held groups are reused with the revised
   expansion mechanism. This is a follow-up, not a pooled repeat of the original pilot.
2. **Refund ledger:** optional payment identity key, validated balance, exact-amount and
   percentage refunds, detached idempotent replay, sequential ledger persistence and
   integrated refund checkout/text. Thirteen held groups cover normal/error/replay and
   complete workflow behavior. Every arm starts with a semantically equivalent loop
   in legacy refundableBalance instead of its unresolved reduce, before assignments
   freeze; the 16 public legacy checks qualify equivalence. This seed choice avoids an
   initial ungrantable block and is not evidence of general callback resolution.
3. **Compiled policy snapshots:** one publisher changes offer/window fields and three
   consumers compile the values in their issued source into archival literals. Publisher
   specifics are in its role directive; consumers must inspect their own issued source
   rather than predict future values. Eight held groups test final agreement, cents,
   payment/stock behavior and frozen results after a fresh child import with perturbed
   config. This is a deliberately dependency-sensitive archival workflow, not a claim
   that ordinary live configuration should be hardcoded. The seed adds two fields and
   leaves legacy policy unchanged. A source-bound, operator-declared compile-time contract
   connects the producer to consumers even when compiled literals have no runtime import.
